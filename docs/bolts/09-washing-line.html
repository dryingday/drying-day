What changed

Days are cloths on a line

The tabs are gone. Three cloths hang from one line, and the chosen day is the large one. It swings when you pick it or tap it again.

The verdict is printed on the cloth

The title and the one sentence sit on white linen. A dry day's cloth is plain. A wet day's cloth is damp at the hem and drips, more when it's worse, and it shakes the water off whenever you tap it. That is the same white-on-sky image as washing on a real line, and it reads in both themes.

The window is a row of washing

Hang-out and bring-in times sit above the ends of a row of pegged washing: shirts, trousers, socks, towels. The row is as long as the window, so its length is the time you have. A day with no window has an empty line and no gap.

The sky does the colour work

Blue for a clear day, grey-blue with a cloud over the sun, dark with rain. The shapes in the sky differ as well as the colour, and the words are always printed.
